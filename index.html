Questions answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What subjects do you offer tutoring for?

Mathematics from KS3 through to A-Level, plus Biology, Chemistry and Physics from KS3 to GCSE. We also offer 11+ preparation and, new for 2026, Chess.

What age groups or school levels do you tutor?

Students at KS3, GCSE and A-Level — whether they need foundational support or are aiming for outstanding exam results.

Do you offer online or in-person tutoring?

Both. Choose whichever suits your schedule and preferred way of learning.

How long are tutoring sessions?

Lessons typically last 1 to 1.5 hours, depending on the student’s needs and the subject.

How much does tutoring cost?

Pricing is per lesson. Discounts apply for multiple lessons per week and for referrals — contact us for current rates.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes — for multiple sessions per week, and referral discounts when an existing student recommends a new pupil.

Can tutoring help with exam preparation?

Absolutely. Lessons strengthen key topics and problem-solving, using practice materials and past exam papers.

Are lessons one-to-one?

Most lessons are one-to-one, so teaching is matched to each learner’s needs. Group options are also available.

How do I book a tutoring session?

Call or WhatsApp 07979 091892, or use our Sign Up form to arrange suitable times.

Can I arrange regular weekly lessons?

Yes. Recurring weekly sessions support consistency and steady academic progress.
